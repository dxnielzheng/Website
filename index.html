01 · RESEARCH

Optical Brain Imaging at mMILAB

Supervised by Dr. Daniel Milej throughout the year, I investigate how Pulsed Speckle Contrast Optical Spectroscopy (Pulsed-SCOS) works and support the Multi-Modal Imaging Lab's transition from traditional Diffuse Correlation Spectroscopy (DCS) to SCOS.

While conventional DCS measures speckle fluctuations through a single-mode fiber one channel at a time, Pulsed-SCOS pairs short near-infrared laser pulses with a CMOS camera sensor to capture millions of speckles simultaneously—improving signal-to-noise ratio for deeper, non-invasive cerebral blood-flow monitoring.
